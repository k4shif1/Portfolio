About

IN  THE
MAKING.

17, turning 18. Studying Mechanical Engineering — Die & Mould Making at Indo Swiss Training Centre by day. Chasing frames, light, and movement every other hour.

No film school. No formal training in this. Just years of watching, studying, and doing — starting with KineMaster edits on a phone at age 11. That obsession never left. It just got sharper.

Got a laptop in 2025. Taught myself After Effects — mograph, typography, transitions, motion principles — all through YouTube and raw curiosity. Shoot on Blackmagic camera app. Studied the aperture triangle, shot composition, and filmography theory the same way: self-directed and obsessive.

The goal? Learning cinematography — the kind that makes you feel it in your chest.

Craft
01 — Camera
Learning Cinematography
Studied shot composition, camera movement, framing, and the aperture triangle. Shooting on Blackmagic camera app — treating every phone frame like a proper set.
02 — Post
Motion Edit
After Effects: mograph, typography animation, transitions, motion principles. Self-taught from raw YouTube deep-dives. Fast learner with an eye for timing and weight.
03 — Eye
Visual Design
Natural instinct for composition, contrast, and visual hierarchy. Influenced by harsh, high-contrast cinema — the kind that doesn't ask for your permission.
04 — Story
Filmography
Deep study of cinematographers, directors, shot language. French New Wave, guerrilla filmmaking, long takes, broken rules — the grammar of film, learned independently.
05 — Type
Typography
Kinetic type, text animation, editorial layout. Treating type as a visual element, not just information. Every letter has weight and intent.
